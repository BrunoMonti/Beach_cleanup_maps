About me

Hi, I'm Bruno. Since September 2023 I've been picking up waste from beaches around Florianópolis (SC, Brazil) and turned the routine into a data project: each cleanup is sorted (qualitative), weighed (quantitative), georeferenced and published here as open data.

The goal is simple: make the invisible visible — measure the waste that reaches the beach, share the numbers, and open the project up for other people's data.

Contributing

Want to contribute data from your own beach? Get in touch — we're setting up a shared database (PostgreSQL/PostGIS) to host cleanups from anywhere.
